Premium Rice Varieties

Explore our wide selection of high-quality rice for every culinary need.

Premium Basmati Rice

Description: Long-grain, highly aromatic rice known for its delicate flavor.

Uses: Biryanis, Pulao, Fried Rice.

Benefits: Low glycemic index, gluten-free, and easy to digest.

Sona Masoori

Description: Medium-grain, lightweight, and aromatic rice widely consumed in South India.

Uses: Daily meals, Pongal, Sweet dishes.

Benefits: Lower in starch, helps in weight management.

Ponni Rice

Description: A popular parboiled rice variety with a high tolerance to overcooking.

Uses: Daily rice, Idli/Dosa batter.

Benefits: High fiber content, good for diabetes management.

Brown Rice

Description: Unpolished whole grain rice that retains its bran and germ layers.

Uses: Healthy salads, side dishes, daily meals for the health-conscious.

Benefits: Rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants; promotes heart health.

Steam Rice

Description: Paddy is steamed before milling, preserving its nutrients and structure.

Uses: Regular dining, catering.

Benefits: Better nutrient retention than raw white rice.

Raw Rice

Description: Uncooked, unprocessed rice directly milled from paddy without steaming.

Uses: Festive dishes, traditional offerings, daily consumption.

Benefits: Pure white appearance, cooks fast and fluffy.
