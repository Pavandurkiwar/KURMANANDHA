About Us

Learn more about our heritage, mission, and commitment to quality.

Our History

Sri Kurmanandha Rice & General Stores was established with a simple vision: to provide the community with the highest quality daily essentials at affordable prices. What started as a small local shop has grown into a trusted name for households across the city.

Our Mission

Our mission is to ensure that every kitchen has access to premium, unadulterated, and nutritious food products. We bridge the gap between quality farmers and end consumers.

Our Vision

We envision a community where healthy eating is accessible and affordable for everyone. We aim to continuously expand our variety while maintaining the trust and quality our customers expect.

Our Commitment

  • 100% Quality Assurance on all products.
  • Fair and transparent pricing.
  • Customer-first approach.
  • Supporting local agriculture.
